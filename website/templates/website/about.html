BEHIND THE WHEEL.

HI, I'M KATIE.

I'm Katie, the face behind High Legh Driving School.

Before becoming a driving instructor, I spent over 10 years working as a solicitor. Although it's very different from what I do now, it taught me to stay calm under pressure, be patient, communicate clearly and support people through situations they find challenging.

I'm also a mum to two girls, so I understand how important it is to know your son or daughter is learning with someone who genuinely cares about their safety. If I were putting my own children into a car with an instructor one day, I'd want to know they were being treated with patience, kindness and respect — and that's exactly how I teach every one of my pupils.

I want my pupils to leave with the confidence to make good decisions, stay calm in difficult situations and drive safely long after they've passed.

Everyone learns differently, so I never expect people to fit into one way of teaching. My lessons are relaxed, supportive and tailored to the individual. Mistakes are part of learning, and I want my pupils to feel comfortable asking questions and building confidence at their own pace.

Whether you're learning to drive yourself or you're a parent looking for the right instructor for your child, I hope you'll find a friendly, supportive environment at High Legh Driving School.
